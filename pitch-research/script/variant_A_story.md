## Say it like this

1. Picture a cleaner in a Leukerbad holiday flat, 10:30, the guests just left. Before she can clean, she bends down for every towel, every sock, every can.
2. Leukerbad had 750,878 nights last season, and 46% of them were in holiday flats, at 80 to 100 francs per checkout clean. This morning, the Valais Chamber of Commerce said Alpine regions like this one are the most exposed to missing staff.
3. From 20 November, Airbnb hosts can no longer ask guests to strip the beds or take out the trash, so that work goes to the cleaner. The best robot on sale costs 2,600 dollars and put a paper ball in its box once in more than 40 tests.
4. What you see is autonomous, at 1× speed, and nobody touches the controller. It sweeps in rows, sees the sock, picks it, and drops it in the laundry bin.
5. Only the grasp is learned; the rest is geometry. On new robot images it found cans 14 of 14 and socks 10 of 12, and the marker on the bin keeps it within 3 cm in simulation.
6. *If measured:* On the real robot, it grasped [__] out of 10. *If not:* We have not measured the grasp rate on the real robot yet, and it has no obstacle avoidance.
7. Flat and hostel operators already pay 80 to 100 francs per checkout, so we offer the robot as a service, starting with a three-month pilot here. It takes the bending down, not the job.
8. We are five students: [names].
9. So at 10:30, the floor is already picked up. We are MAKAI, and we ask for three months in real Leukerbad flats.
